Transforming Manual VAT Compliance into Self-Serve Platform for Small Businesses

Avalara's managed VAT returns product was a service-led business that couldn't scale. Every new customer required hiring a new agent. This is how we transformed the model entirely, giving customers the tools to manage compliance themselves.

£7.1M Revenue (from £3.7M)
1,600 Customers migrated
0 Compliance failures
92% Revenue growth over 5 years

Context: A Service That Couldn't Scale

Avalara's Managed VAT Returns product helped small businesses handle multi-country VAT compliance in Europe. When I joined as Senior Product Manager in May 2019:

  • Revenue: £3.7M
  • Model: human VAT agents doing manual data entry and compliance work
  • Customer count: 1,600 businesses
  • Pain point: growth was capped by headcount; customer experience was poor

The Problem: Four Constraints

The service-led model had fundamental constraints that couldn't be optimised away:

  • Growth limitation: each new customer required hiring a VAT agent. Revenue was directly proportional to headcount.
  • Poor customer experience: businesses had to email data to agents for manual processing. Turnaround: days. Error rate: high.
  • Compliance risk: no audit trail, manual workflows, no end-user visibility into their own compliance status.
  • Regulatory pressure: GDPR meant we couldn't properly handle customer data with no encryption or multi-tenancy.

The Strategic Insight

Rather than optimise the service model, we asked a different question: what if we could give customers the tools to do this themselves?

Sometimes the constraint isn't product features — it's business model. We could have optimised the service (hire more agents, be faster, be cheaper). Instead, we transformed the model entirely.

This was radical for a service-led business. It meant admitting the service model had limits, investing heavily in product before seeing financial return, risking disruption to existing agent-based revenue, and building a completely new way of working.

The Solution: Customer Portal

I partnered with Product Design and Engineering to lead the design and build of a self-serve customer portal that fundamentally changed how businesses interacted with VAT compliance:

Self-Service Data Submission

Businesses could upload transaction data directly, eliminating the email-and-wait workflow entirely.

Automated VAT Calculations

System calculated VAT obligations across jurisdictions, handling different rules, thresholds, and filing frequencies.

Compliance Reporting

Automatic generation of VAT returns ready for filing, with full audit trail for customers and regulators.

GDPR-Compliant Architecture

Multi-tenant architecture with encryption and data isolation, meeting regulatory requirements properly.

The Migration Challenge

This wasn't a new product launch. We had to migrate 1,600 existing customers from an agent-driven process to self-service, over 8 months, without losing them or breaking compliance.

Execution approach

  • Phased onboarding: gradually move customers to the portal, providing support during transition
  • Agent transformation: agents shifted from data entry to compliance specialists, supporting customers through the portal
  • Backward compatibility: portal coexisted with the manual process; no forced migration
  • Change management: training, communication, and support to help customers succeed
  • Risk management: ensured zero compliance failures during the entire transition

Migrating 1,600 customers without a single compliance failure required careful planning and execution at every step. There was no margin for error when dealing with tax obligations.

Financial Impact

The transformation delivered structural improvement to the business model:

  • Pre-transformation (2019): £3.7M revenue, capped by agent headcount
  • Post-transformation (2024): £7.1M revenue
  • Growth: 92% over 5 years (approximately 17% YoY CAGR)
  • Efficiency: same agent team could serve 2–3x more customers
  • Customer satisfaction improved through visibility, speed, and autonomy
  • Operational cost reduced per customer

Revenue growth came without proportional headcount increase — proving the structural advantage of the platform model over the service model.

Key Learnings

Clarity on the real problem

We didn't just complain about headcount constraints. We identified the root issue — a service-led model — and solved it structurally. The constraint wasn't resources; it was the business model itself.

User empathy drives transformation

We understood what customers really wanted: visibility, speed, and autonomy. Not just what agents were providing (manual processing). This reframe changed the entire product direction.

Technical excellence in migration

VAT compliance is genuinely complex — edge cases, jurisdictional variations, regulatory requirements. Our system had to handle all of this with zero tolerance for error.

Change management matters

Technology alone doesn't transform a business. We invested equally in customer communication, agent reskilling, and phased rollout to make the transition work for everyone.
